# Features

Ascendancy could implement a plethora of features related to magic and gods, but an important fact to keep in mind is that they have to be useful to the player.

The most important features are listed below.

### Player Stats

Key stats introduced by Ascendancy:

- True Name
- Lives
- Execution Points (ExP)
- Resurrection Count
- Levels

Running out of Lives "Void Bans" the player, sending them to their beds equivalent location in the Void Realm. They stay trapped there until another living player rescues them through a ritual, relic, arcana, or other divine nonsense.

Execution Points come from PvP or PvE (configurable). Enough ExP levels the player up. At Level 50, they can perform a Resurrection Ritual to "Prestige" and gain new permanent perks.

#### Resurrection Perks

Prestiged players gain powerful abilities, like:

- Detecting when someone holds an innate item linked to them.
- Knowing when their True Name is spoken.
- Instantly teleporting to someone attempting mischief with their name.
- Being able to shorten or replace incantations all together.

#### True Names

Each player gets a unique innate name.

- Can be freely rerolled only if no other player has ever heard it.
- If the secret’s out, changing it requires a ritual, boss fight, or other high-stakes nonsense.
- Names are censored in chat unless the listener already knows it or is within a configurable proximity.

#### Factions

Players of prestige 1 and higher can start factions.

Faction leaders gain the benefit of receiving less damage from faction members and gaining extra health when enough faction members are nearby. They can also set up PVP rules in a faction, i.e. rules of engagement, like "Player A and B cannot attack each other" or "Player C is protected from PVP within the faction overall".

Faction members get access to exclusive items like, among others, a `spectral whistle` which lets them announce a message to all other members regardless of distance.

### Magic

Players find Torn Spell Books in loot chests. These teach keywords or incantations.  
Write them into a book, sign it, and boom: you’ve got a spell scroll. Right-click to cast and pray you don’t explode.

### Rituals

Rituals range from simple soul-fire offerings to full candle-lined circles.  
They can:

- Consume items, mobs, or both.
- Produce items, entities, effects, or events.
- Be instant or sustained, reacting to what happens in the ritual area as long as the structure stays intact.

Basically, crafting table meets summoning circle meets OSHA violation.

### Divinity

Players can assign incantations to entities, objects, or concepts—e.g., _"the messenger belonging to \<player\>"_. As beings grow in power, these incantations may become references to literal gods.

Ascendancy itself adds certain higher existences which can be prayed to. For example:

- The Primordial Abyss
  ```
  The Abyss deep beneath this world
  You are the origin and bane of all life
  The end of creation and destruction
  ```
  (Illustratory incantation)

Since incantations are a pain, these too can be put in a signed book to auto-read quickly.

#### Prayers

Multi-step incantations or ritual actions used to request favors from whatever divine or semi-divine thing the incantation points to.

Whether it listens… is another story.

#### Shikigami

Players can contract other players and certain entities to become "shikigami" (of the contracting player).

By accepting such a contract, the contractee gains and loses several abilities:

- Lives no longer apply to shikigami, as they are shared inward from the owner. (Shikigami dying won't affect the contractor)
- Resurrection and Levels are shared both ways
- Innate names become unnecessary between the two, they can use usernames
- The shikigami can hide within its contractors' shadow to travel with them easily and without being seen by others
- The contractor can summon their contractees at any time

### Void Realm

A multilayered dimension made of stacked worlds.  
Players fall in when they touch the void, arriving on the first layer. From there, they can:

- Use rituals to travel between layers.
- Drop into the void again to reach deeper ones.
- Encounter unique structures, bosses, and traversal opportunities.

Each layer alters the Void : Overworld travel ratio, starting at 1:16 and doubling each tier.

### Bosses

Bosses can appear via:

- Rituals
- Divinity incantations
- Structures
- Summoning items
- Or just naturally existing to ruin someone’s day

Defeating them rewards Relics, Arcanas, or progress toward Resurrection/Levels.

### Innate Names (Commands)

True names allow highly targeted commands like:

- "_\<name\> I bestow you health_"
- "_\<name\> close your eyes_"

Blessings, curses, boons, debuffs... if you can phrase it, you can probably weaponize it.

### Innate Items

Players can bind abilities to items. These items are:

- Soulbound to the owner
- Droppable only as ritual-grade sacrifices
- Dangerous tools for tracking or targeting that player

Effects can include:

- Returning to the owner on death
- Casting spells
- Pathfinding to locations
- Basically any power you can cram into an item without summoning a lawyer

### Races

On first join, players choose from configurable races (Origins).  
Each race has unique traits and belongs to either:

- Soul Beings
- Void Beings

### Relics

Powerful passive, active, or equipment-based items.  
Rules:

- Players can equip only one, others become "itemized relics."
- Cannot be stored in containers.
- Dropped only by bestowing them via someone’s True Name.
- Lost upon death (yeeted into the void).
- A special NPC may spawn near Void Realm structures to store a relic safely.

Examples:

- Manasense – passive dodge chance.
- Lightshot – long-range grappling ability in item form.
- Forbidden Blade – modifies any weapon held by the user to inflict bonus damage based on enemy mana.
- Abyssal Blade – sword item which opens and destroys dimensional gateways.

### Arcanas

Think "elite magic spells."  
Dropped exclusively by bosses, significantly stronger than normal spells, and share a global cooldown determined by the last Arcana used.

### Artifacts

Player-crafted innate items with abilities or spells engraved.  
Not soulbound.  
Players can carry as many as their questionable life choices allow.  
Drawbacks vary: limited uses, weakness debuffs, swapped breathing types (air ↔ water), and other cursed side effects.
