# The Void Realm

The Void Realm is a custom dimension stack that serves as a punishment and a challenge for players who fall out of the world. It consists of three distinct layers, each with its own mechanics.

## Layers

### 1. The Abyss
The first layer of the Void Realm.
*   **Entry**: Players who fall into the void in the Overworld (and are not in combat) are transported here.
*   **Exit**: Falling into the void here drops you to **Oblivion**.

### 2. Oblivion
The second, deeper layer.
*   **Entry**: Falling from The Abyss.
*   **Exit**: Falling into the void here drops you to **Reflection of Self**.

### 3. Reflection of Self
The final, infinite layer.
*   **Entry**: Falling from Oblivion.
*   **Mechanics**:
    *   **Wraparound**: The world wraps around itself. Walking too far in one direction will bring you back to the other side.
    *   **Loop**: Falling into the void here simply teleports you back to the top of the Reflection layer.

## Mechanics

### Entering the Void
*   **Safety Net**: If you have high health or are not in combat, falling into the Overworld void will save you by transporting you to the Abyss instead of killing you.
*   **Items**: Dropped items that fall into the void are also transported to the Abyss.

### Escaping (Bedrock Breaker)
To return to the Overworld, you must ascend.
*   **Bedrock Breaking**: When you reach the top of the Void Realm hierarchy and attempt to transition back to the Overworld, a "Bedrock Breaker" event initiates.
*   **Tunneling**: You will be tunneled up through the bottom of the Overworld, breaking bedrock and deepslate in your path.
*   **Protection**: During this ascent, kinetic damage (elytra crashes) and suffocation damage are disabled to ensure a safe return.

### Death & Respawn
*   **Lives**: If you have lives remaining (from the life system), you may respawn normally.
*   **Void Respawn**: If you die without lives, you will respawn in **The Abyss**, trapping you in the cycle until you can escape.

### Environmental Effects
*   **Fall Damage**: Fall damage is disabled throughout the Void Realm layers.
