# Ascendancy Spells Documentation

This document outlines all the spells available in the Ascendancy plugin.

## Common Spells

### Aeroburst
**Incantations:** *Aeroburst*, *Gale Shot*, *Spiritus*
**Cooldown:** 8 seconds
**Effect:** Launches a powerful gust of wind (Wind Charge) that knocks back entities and can be used for mobility.

### Fireball
**Incantations:** *Fire Ball*, *Fire Sphere*, *Ignis*
**Cooldown:** 10 seconds
**Effect:** Launches a fireball that explodes on impact.

### Ventus
**Incantations:** *Ventus*, *Dash*, *Wind*
**Cooldown:** 5 seconds
**Effect:** Launches the caster forward in the direction they are looking.

## Uncommon Spells

### Expelliarmus
**Incantations:** *Expelliarmus*, *Disarm*
**Cooldown:** 15 seconds
**Effect:** Disarms the target entity. If the target is a player or a mob holding an item, that item is dropped to the ground.

### Fulmen
**Incantations:** *Fulmen*, *Lightning Strike*, *Thunder*
**Cooldown:** 15 seconds
**Effect:** Strikes the target block with lightning.

### Glacius
**Incantations:** *Glacius*, *Stirps Glacialis*, *Freeze*
**Cooldown:** 15 seconds
**Effect:** Freezes the target entity for 7 seconds, applying Slowness II.

### Tractum
**Incantations:** *Tractum*, *Accio*, *Venite*
**Cooldown:** 10 seconds
**Effect:** Pulls the target entity towards the caster.

### Vonszol
**Incantations:** *Vonszol*, *Almighty Pull*
**Cooldown:** 15 seconds
**Effect:** Pulls all nearby living entities (within 5.5 blocks) towards the location where the spell was cast.

## Rare Spells

### Recall
**Incantations:** *Recall*
**Cooldown:** 20 seconds
**Effect:** Teleports the caster to the location stored in a held Lodestone Compass.

### Sanatio
**Incantations:** *Sanatio*, *Divine Light*
**Cooldown:** 30 seconds
**Effect:** Heals the caster for 4 hearts (8 health points).
