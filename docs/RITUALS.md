# Ascendancy Rituals Documentation

This document outlines all the rituals available in the Ascendancy plugin.

## Basic Rituals

### Ritual of Levitation
**Catalyst:** Any
**Sacrifices:**
*   Feather (1) OR Chicken (1)
**Effect:** Grants Levitation to nearby players. Duration and strength depend on the amount of sacrifices.

### Ritual of the Wolf
**Catalyst:** Any
**Sacrifices:**
*   Bone (5 per wolf)
*   Rotten Flesh (1 per wolf)
**Effect:** Spawns tamed wolves that belong to the invoker.

## Intermediate Rituals

### Return to Death
**Catalyst:** Any
**Sacrifices:**
*   Recovery Compass (1)
**Effect:** Teleports the invoker to their last death location.

### Home Coming
**Catalyst:** Any
**Sacrifices:**
*   Compass (1)
**Effect:** Teleports nearby players to the invoker's respawn location (or world spawn).

### Ritual of Sunshine
**Catalyst:** Any
**Sacrifices:**
*   Sunflower (1) OR Gold Ingot (1)
**Effect:** Sets the time to morning and clears any rain or thunder.

## Advanced Rituals

### Ritual of the Anchor
**Catalyst:** Any
**Sacrifices:**
*   Lodestone (1)
*   Compass (1)
*   Ender Pearl (5)
**Effect:** Creates a Lodestone Compass bound to the ritual's location.

### Soul Sending
**Catalyst:** End Crystal (or sacrifice Ghast Tear)
**Sacrifices:**
*   Name Tag (1) (with innate name engraved)
*   Ghast Tear (1) (if catalyst is not End Crystal)
**Effect:** Teleports the invoker to the player whose innate name is on the Name Tag.

## Master Rituals

### Void Revealing
**Catalyst:** Wither Rose (or sacrifice one)
**Sacrifices:**
*   Wither Rose (1) (if catalyst is not Wither Rose)
*   Player (1)
**Effect:** Reveals the Void Realm (details depend on implementation/config).
