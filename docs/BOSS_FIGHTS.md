# Boss Fight Progression

Ascendancy introduces a progression system where access to high-tier equipment is gated behind boss kills. You must prove your worth by defeating powerful foes to wield the strongest materials.

## Progression Tiers

### Diamond Tier
To use Diamond equipment, you must defeat a specific number of bosses.
*   **Requirement**: Configurable (Default: Check `config.yml`)
*   **Unlocked Items**: Diamond Armor, Tools, and Weapons.

### Netherite Tier
To use Netherite equipment, you must defeat even more bosses, including those from the Nether.
*   **Requirement**: Configurable (Default: Check `config.yml`)
*   **Unlocked Items**: Netherite Armor, Tools, and Weapons.

## Supported Bosses

The plugin recognizes various bosses from Vanilla Minecraft and popular datapacks.

### Overworld
*   **Elder Guardian** (Vanilla)
*   **Warden** (Vanilla)

### The Nether
*   **Wither** (Vanilla)
*   **Hovering Inferno** (Incendium Datapack)

### The End
*   **Ender Dragon** (Vanilla)
*   **Empress of Light** (Stellarity Datapack)

## Mechanics

*   **Kill Credit**: Players within a configurable radius of the boss death will receive credit for the kill.
*   **Locking**: If you attempt to use an item you haven't unlocked, the plugin will prevent you from using it.
