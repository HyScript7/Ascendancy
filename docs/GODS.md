# Gods and Prayers

These are special rituals performed by chanting specific phrases.

## The Primordial Abyss

* The Abyss that transcends the realms
* You are the darkness deep beneath this world
* The bane and source of all life and creation

### Abysmal Ascent (Protection)

**Incantations:**
*   "I beg for your attention"
*   "I beg of you to hide my true name within the void"

**Effect:** Grants protection to the invoker's true name, hiding it within the void.

### Abysmal Ascent (Travel)

**Incantations:**
*   "I pray for your attention"
*   "I pray for you to let me leave this empty realm"

**Effect:** Ascends the invoker to a higher Void Realm layer (e.g., Oblivion -> Abyss).

### Abysmal Descent (Travel)

**Incantations:**
*   "I pray for your attention"
*   "I wish for you to pull me into your embrace"

**Effect:** Descends the invoker to a lower Void Realm layer (e.g., Abyss -> Oblivion).
